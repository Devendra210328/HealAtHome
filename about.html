WHO WE ARE

PhysioVertex is a premier home-based physiotherapy service provider in Mumbai, dedicated to bringing expert rehabilitation care directly to your doorstep. Founded with a mission to make professional physiotherapy accessible, convenient, and effective, we specialize in neurological, orthopaedic, and geriatric rehabilitation.

Our approach is simple yet powerful: personalized, one-on-one care in the comfort of your own home. We understand that recovery is not just about physical healing—it's about restoring confidence, independence, and quality of life. That's why we've built a service that combines clinical excellence with compassionate, patient-centered care.

Whether you're recovering from a stroke, managing chronic pain, rehabilitating after surgery, or seeking support for age-related mobility issues, PhysioVertex is here to guide you every step of the way. Our certified specialist brings years of experience, state-of-the-art techniques, and a genuine commitment to your recovery.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality physiotherapy services that empower individuals to achieve optimal recovery and wellness in the comfort of their homes. We are committed to delivering personalized care that addresses each patient's unique needs, ensuring faster recovery and improved quality of life.
