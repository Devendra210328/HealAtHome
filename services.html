WHY CHOOSE HOME PHYSIOTHERAPY IN MUMBAI?

For patients across Mumbai, getting to a physiotherapy clinic can be one of the biggest barriers to recovery. Whether you live in the congested lanes of South Mumbai, the busy Western suburbs of Andheri and Bandra, or the growing townships of Navi Mumbai and Thane — commuting with pain, limited mobility, or a wheelchair is extremely difficult.

PhysioVertex's home physiotherapy service in Mumbai eliminates this barrier completely. Our certified physiotherapist comes to your home with all necessary equipment, provides one-on-one attention for the full session, and trains you in exercises that work specifically in your home environment — your bathroom, your bedroom, your stairs.

Research published in the Journal of Physiotherapy shows that home-based rehabilitation produces equal or better outcomes compared to clinic-based physiotherapy, especially for elderly patients and those recovering from stroke or surgery. Patients receiving home physiotherapy also show higher compliance with exercise programs because the physiotherapist can customize exercises around the patient's daily routine and home setup.
